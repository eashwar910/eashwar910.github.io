## Overview

A VGG16-based real-time system for detecting rotten fruit, built during a remote internship
with **Dr. Ken Kahn** (Retired Lecturer, University of Oxford).

## Role

Developed the computer vision pipeline using convolutional neural networks and transfer
learning, fine-tuning a VGG16 backbone for the classification task. Delivered a functional
solution demonstrating image classification and model fine-tuning end to end.

## Stack

- VGG16 (transfer learning) for the classifier backbone
- Convolutional neural networks for real-time inference

_Aug – Oct 2023 · University of Oxford (remote)_
