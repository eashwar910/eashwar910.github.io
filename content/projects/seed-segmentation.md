## Overview

A U-Net based **semantic segmentation** pipeline for automated seed analysis, built as a
Student Research Intern at the University of Nottingham, Malaysia.

## Role

Designed and implemented the segmentation pipeline to enable precise **radicle and plumule
measurement**, supporting quality-assurance workflows in seed distribution companies. Applied
deep learning and image-processing techniques to achieve high-accuracy segmentation on
microscope slide datasets.

## Stack

- U-Net architecture for semantic segmentation
- Deep learning + classical image processing on microscope imagery

_Jul – Sep 2025 · University of Nottingham, Malaysia_
